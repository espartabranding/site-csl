TITAN POLYBLEND®: THE HIGH-YIELD ROPE

Titan Polyblend rope is made with CSL’s exclusive technology and is the highest-yield option among synthetic fibres. Its strength and durability are far superior to polyethylene, polypropylene, PET and other copolymer ropes, bringing much more stability and safety to operations.

The highest-yield option among synthetic fibres. See the difference for yourself.

On average, a Polyblend rope has the same strength as:

  • A PET rope 4 mm larger in diameter
  • A PE rope 3 mm larger in diameter
  • A PP rope 2 mm larger in diameter
