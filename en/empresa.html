Who we are

Cordoaria São Leopoldo

Cordoaria São Leopoldo Original Ltda is a genuinely Brazilian company, internationally recognised as one of the world’s leading manufacturers of synthetic fibre ropes.

It began its history making natural fibre ropes (hemp, jute and sisal) and electrical cables. This was a very important period for the company, as it marked its entry into the fishing market, first with laid sisal ropes and later with synthetic fibres as the main raw materials for its products.

Developing new products and investing heavily in state-of-the-art machinery were major challenges, but they allowed CSL to reach technologically complex markets such as the mooring of oil tankers and large cargo ships.

Today the company is prepared to meet any need for high-performance ropes, in the most varied applications that call for them.
