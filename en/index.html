Products by construction

Constructions

The construction defines shape, flexibility, elongation and how the rope takes splices.

3-strand laid

3 strands · Z or S lay

Three strands laid to the right (Z lay, CSL standard) or to the left (S lay). Evenly twisted yarns give a balanced, firm and flexible rope. Also available in 4 strands.

8-strand plaited

4 S strands + 4 Z strands

Square shape. Does not distort and has no residual torque. Easy handling, controlled elongation and good tensile strength.

12-strand braided

6 S strands + 6 Z strands

Round shape with no residual torque. Great flexibility and excellent abrasion and tensile strength.

16-strand braided

hollow core

Ideal construction for making eyes and for gill nets with lead sticks inside the braid.

Marine double braid

50% cover · 50% core

A long-pitch braided core controls elongation and preserves strength; the circular cover protects against abrasion. Follows the OCIMF guidelines.

Circular braid

80% core · 20% cover

Long-pitch braided core for higher tensile strength and controlled elongation; circular cover with high wear performance.

Sport double braid

polyester and HMPE

For all kinds of halyards and sheets and for winch work. Old acquaintances of Brazilian sailors.
